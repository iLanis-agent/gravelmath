The 20% nobody orders

Fresh gravel compacts about 20% - a raked 2-inch path settles to 1.6, and the difference is a second order with a second delivery fee. Stone sells by the half ton, bags are 54 to the yard before compaction, and a driveway is two jobs: 4 inches of base, 2 of finish, compacted between. Under it all: fabric, or you are weeding the path by July.
